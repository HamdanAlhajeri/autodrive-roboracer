# Analysis of the 24 Nov floor run, and suggested changes (25 Nov)

Data: `jetson-car-days` branch, day 2 (floor-run probe and SLAM trace, ring map). The Jetson was off, so everything here is from recorded data and the code. Nothing was tried on the car.

## What the floor run shows

1. **The car did not follow the speed command.** The command was 0.8 m/s from t = 12 s to t = 39 s. Over that time the motor speed (erpm) stayed at about 25 to 670 in magnitude, against about 3700 when the car really drove at 0.8 m/s. Measured speed from SLAM was 0.02 to 0.18 m/s for the first 25 s. After t = 39 s it reached the commanded speed within seconds. The same thing happened at t = 50 to 60 s and again at the end (command 0.07 to 0.3 m/s, no motion). This looks like a motor or VESC start-up or speed-loop problem, not a planner problem.
2. **Two things seen once, not yet explained:** the erpm sign was positive for the first few seconds (the gain was negated on 24 Nov), and the speed jumped to 1.08 m/s at t = 58 s, above the 0.8 command.
3. **Steering sits at its limit in every sharp turn.** The turn radius computed from the SLAM path is 1.40 m again and again, which is the minimum the profile allows (wheelbase 0.314 m, 0.22 rad max). The turns the car stalled in were about 160 to 175 degree left arcs.
4. **The corridor is narrow for that radius.** The rejected ring map (5.5 x 5.1 m) has a corridor about 1.1 to 1.7 m wide (median about 1.45 m) around a small island. A U-turn at 1.4 m radius needs the car to use almost the whole corridor and swing wide first. Follow-the-Gap reacts to what is in front, so it turns in late, hits the inside wall and stalls. This is an estimate from the map image, not a measurement on the car.

## Changed today

- `stall.py`: a stall monitor. If the car is commanded 0.3 m/s or more and moves less than a quarter of it for 6 s, the session stops with the numbers in the reason. `timing.stall_timeout_s` sets the time (0 turns it off). 204 tests pass.
- Not tried on the car. Expect it to fire on the slow start seen on 24 Nov until the motor start-up problem is fixed.

## Suggested next changes (in order)

1. **Find why the motor does not start.** With wheels off and the supervisor mapping ramp, log duty cycle, motor current and erpm. Read the VESC settings with VESC Tool (motor type, speed PID, current limits, minimum erpm).
2. **Measure the real steering angles** with the protractor, then decide: widen the limit, or an asymmetric limit (left reaches about 0.22 rad, right about 0.35 rad).
3. **Drive the lap clockwise** if the right side reaches further, once item 2 confirms it.
4. **Mapping speed 0.4 to 0.5 m/s for the first autonomous lap.** At 0.8 the late turn-in is worse and the map is rejected sooner.
5. **A wider or less tight test track** (about 2 m corridor) for the first mapped lap.
6. **Keep all caps as they are** (1.0 m/s, 0.25 s stale limit, 0.3 m wall-step rule).
